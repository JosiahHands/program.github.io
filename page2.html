Instructions

It's not complicated

People are always trying to make stuff complicated, if that's you, just relax dude.
I use a regular propane grill with basic seasoning, I just have a few tips that make
your steak consistently better.

Step 1

Get your steaks out and let them come to room temperature

step 2

If your steaks are not premium, rinse them under cold water

Step 3

Preheat the grill on high to 400°F and clean the grates

Step 4

If its the heat of the summer, the grill should be 325-375, but if its the cool of the winter the grill should be 375-425 (in between where applicable).
Note: this is a general guideline for the texas panhandle, adjust as needed.

Step 5

Throw the steaks on the grill and add whatever seasoning you like + olive oil.
I like Trooper Seasoning or salt&pepper.
Note: your grill distribution is important, if its at the front it will be
closer to blue rare and at the back it will be closer to medium rare. If want all steaks
to be cooked the same, swap the front/back steaks when you flip them

Step 6

For 1 inch thick steaks, cook for 4 minutes on each side. adjust linearly for thickness.
If it's bone in add 25%

Step 7

Remove the steaks and let them rest 2 minutes before serving.

Summary

As you can see its not complicated at all. Here's a summery of the few points that I think are important
(in order of importance):

  1. consistent timeline - insures predictability

  2. temperature adjustment - as the weather changes this is crucial

  3. using olive oil - tenderizes the steak and enhances the flavor
